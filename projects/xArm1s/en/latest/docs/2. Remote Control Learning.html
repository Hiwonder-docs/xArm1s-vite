2.Remote Control Learning ​

2.1 Wireless Controller ​

2.1.1 Getting Ready ​

Step 1: Insert the wireless controller receiver into the controller board, as shown below:

Step 2: Prepare your own 2pcs AAA batteries and insert them into the battery slot. Please pay attention to the positive and negative poles.

2.1.2 Device Connection ​

Step 1: Turn on the xArm 1S robotic arm.

Step 2: Turn on the wireless controller. The red and green LEDs will flash.

Step 3: The robotic arm will automatically pair with the wireless controller after a few seconds. The red and green LED will be steady on after pairing. If there is only the red light or green light when you turn on, please press the “MODE” key.

Step 4: If the connection fails, please turn off the robotic arm and wireless controller, and then try again following the above steps.

NOTE

Sleep Mode: If the robotic arm is not connected within 30 seconds after turning on the handle switch, or if no operation is performed on the handle within 5 minutes after connection, the handle will enter sleep mode. Pressing the "Start" key can restart the wireless controller.

2.1.3 Mode Instruction ​

There are action group mode and single servo mode for the wireless controller. The default mode is action group mode.

Action group mode: Press the key to run the corresponding actions.(For customers who buy the unassembled version, please download the action groups into the controller in advance.)

Single servo mode: Press the key to rotate each servo.

Mode switching method: Press the “SELECT” first and then “START” until hearing a “Beep” sound. The mode will change from action group mode to single servo mode.

2.1.4 Button Instruction ​

The method of pressing the combination key is to press and hold "SELECT" first, and then press the corresponding key.

## 2.2 App Control

2.2.1 APP Installation ​

Android user: Please transfer the installed pack to your smart phone to install.

iOS user: Please search “Wonderbot” in the App Store.

2.2.2 Device Connection ​

NOTE

  1. Please turn on the Bluetooth and GPS service before opening the APP.

  2. Please use the Bluetooth button in the APP to pair and connect with the device. Do not pair with the key in the phone settings.

  3. This lesson takes the iOS version of the app as an example. The system version requirements: Android 7.0 or above, iOS 9.0 or above.

Step 1: Turn on the robotic arm.

Step 2: Open the Wonderbot APP to the interface as shown below.

If your interface is different, please click icon and “Settings” to choose the “xArm”.

Step 3: Click the flashing Bluetooth icon on the interface to search for the device. Please select the “xArm” later.

Step 4: Wait for a while, the Bluetooth icon will stop flashing and stay on. The "Bluetooth is connected" prompt will appear.

2.2.3 Mode Instruction ​

Mode 1 Model Remote Control： Combine the two-dimensional screen with the button to control the movement of the robotic arm easily.

Mode 2 Create Actions: Under this mode, you can program actions, debug, and record the data of the robotic arm.

Mode 3 My Action: You can store, display, and delete the custom actions.

For the detailed instruction of programming in the APP, please go to the folder “Expansion Lesson/Smart Phone APP Programming”.

2.2.4 Button Instruction ​

  1. Under Mode 1 Model Remote Control, the interface is as follows:

  1. On the right side, there is a 2D animation of xArm 1S. You can drag to control the movement, but also check the stance after rotation. Buttons on the left side support controlling the rotation of the gripper, wrist, and bottom.

  2. Click “Action Group” to execute the action groups that are already downloaded into the robotic arm. For more details, please go to the folder “3. xArm 1S PC Software and APP Learning/Lesson 3 APP Custom Control”.

  3. Click “Operation Instruction” for the operating method.

  1. Click “Back to Start” to call all the servos back to the middle position.

2.3 Mouse Control ​

The control board of the xArm 1S robotic arm can analyze the mouse action information, and transfer the obtained data to the single-chip microcomputer through SPI for processing. Then the corresponding servos will execute the action.

2.3.1 Getting Ready ​

Step 1: Please prepare your own mouse. If the wireless remote control receiver is in the controller board, don’t forget to take it out.

Step 2: Connect the mouse to the controller, as shown below:

2.3.2 Device Connection ​

Step 1: Turn on the robotic arm.

Step 2: Please wait for a second. The robotic arm will automatically pair with the mouse.
